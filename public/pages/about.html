About MAP-USA

Learn more about our mission, the people behind the project, and why pollution education matters.

Our Mission

MAP-USA was created to make local air quality data more accessible, understandable, and useful for students, educators, researchers, and community members. Our goal is to support environmental learning through clear, engaging, and interactive tools.

By bringing together data visualization, GIS mapping, and education-focused resources, we hope to help users better understand the pollution patterns affecting South Atlanta communities.

MAP-USA Team
Georgia Tech capstone students working with the Center for Sustainable Communities to build an accessible GIS-based educational pollution data portal.

Why This Is Important

Air pollution can have major impacts on health, learning, and everyday life, yet it is often difficult for communities to access local data in a way that feels meaningful.

This platform is meant to bridge that gap by turning complex pollution information into something students and communities can actually explore, understand, and use.
