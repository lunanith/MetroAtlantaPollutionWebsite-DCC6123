About the Project

Making local pollution data easier to explore and understand

MAP-USA is a GIS-based educational platform designed to help students, educators, researchers, and community members interact with local air pollution information in a way that feels clear, approachable, and meaningful.

GIS Mapping Education Community Access

Our Focus

Turning complex environmental data into accessible tools that support learning, awareness, and community understanding.

✦

Our Mission

MAP-USA was created to make local air quality data more accessible, understandable, and useful for students, educators, researchers, and community members.

Our goal is to support environmental learning through clear, engaging, and interactive tools that help users better understand pollution patterns affecting South Atlanta communities.

↗

What the Platform Brings Together

Educational Resources

Supporting classroom learning and public understanding through approachable content.

GIS Mapping

Helping users connect environmental data to specific neighborhoods and local spaces.

Data Visualization

Presenting pollution information in ways that feel easier to read and interpret.

Why This Matters

Air pollution can affect health, learning, and everyday quality of life, yet local environmental data is often difficult to access or understand.

This platform helps bridge that gap by turning complex information into something students and communities can actually explore, understand, and use.

☼

What We Hope Users Gain

Awareness

A clearer picture of local environmental conditions and patterns.

Confidence

More comfort interacting with maps, graphs, and pollution-related concepts.

Connection

A stronger understanding of how data relates to real communities and lived experiences.
